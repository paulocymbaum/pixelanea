# Pixelanea — student handout

**Pixel art on your computer · no account needed**

## Getting started

1. Open **Pixelanea** from the application menu.
2. Click **Open existing project** and choose the template file your teacher shared (`blank-starter.pixelanea`).
   - Or click **Start blank** to make your own canvas.
3. Pick a color from the palette on the right.
4. Click on the canvas to paint pixels.

## Tools you'll use

| Tool | What it does | Shortcut |
|------|--------------|----------|
| Pencil | Paint pixels | `B` |
| Eraser | Fix mistakes | `E` |
| Eyedropper | Pick a color from your art | `I` |
| Undo | Undo last change | `Ctrl+Z` |

## Saving your work

1. **File → Save As**
2. Choose a name ending in `.pixelanea`
3. When the status bar says **All changes saved**, your file is ready to copy to USB or email

## Mini challenges (pick one)

- **Icon** — 16×16 heart, star, or sword (max 4 colors)
- **Character** — 32×32 simple face
- **Animation** — 8-frame blinking eye (ask your teacher about Duplicate frames)

## Need help?

- Press `?` in the editor for the full shortcut list
- Ask your teacher if the file picker doesn't open — you can type the full file path instead

---

*Pixelanea keeps everything on your computer. Nothing is uploaded to the cloud.*
